Manzil / AddressQo'qon shahri, Turkiston ko'chasi, 212-uy
Telefon / Phone

O'zbekcha

Men, yuqorida ko'rsatilgan do'kon egasi, «ZYFF» mobil ilovasi va zyff.uz veb-saytiga quyidagilarga ruxsat beraman:

  1. Do'konimning nomi, logotipi, manzili va mahsulotlarim (rasm, nom, tavsif, narx) ZYFF platformasida ko'rsatilishi;
  2. Xaridorlarning do'konimga mo'ljallangan buyurtmalarini ZYFF orqali qabul qilinishi va menga yetkazilishi.

Tasdiqlayman: platformaga joylanadigan barcha mahsulot rasmlari, nomlari va tavsiflari menga tegishli yoki men ulardan foydalanish huquqiga egaman; ushbu kontentni ZYFF o'zi olmagan — men do'kon egasi panelidan o'zim joylayman va boshqaraman; ZYFF menga yoki xaridorlarga ilovadan foydalanish uchun to'lov belgilamaydi va to'lovlarni o'zi qabul qilmaydi.

Ushbu ruxsat men uni yozma ravishda bekor qilmagunimcha amal qiladi.

English

I, the owner of the store named above, hereby authorise the mobile application ZYFF and the website zyff.uz to:

  1. Display my store's name, logo, address and my products (images, names, descriptions and prices) on the ZYFF platform;
  2. Receive customer orders addressed to my store through ZYFF and forward them to me.

I confirm that all product images, names and descriptions published on the platform belong to me or that I hold the rights to use them; that this content was not collected by ZYFF — I upload and manage it myself through the store-owner panel; and that ZYFF charges neither me nor the customers any fee for access to the application, and does not collect payments on my behalf.

This authorisation remains valid until I withdraw it in writing.

Imzo / Signature
Abdurashidov Sardor 		Muhr — agar bo'lsa
Stamp — if any
